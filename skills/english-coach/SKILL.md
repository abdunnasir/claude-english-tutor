---

## Instructions

When given an English sentence to check:

1. Review it for grammar, word-choice, and register issues.
2. For each issue found, list:
   - The problematic word/phrase
   - Why it's wrong (brief, teach the reasoning)
   - The corrected word/phrase
3. Prefer natural native phrasing over textbook-perfect rewrites.
4. End with a line **Corrected sentence:** followed by the full corrected sentence.
5. If no issues, say so and repeat the sentence as the corrected version.
